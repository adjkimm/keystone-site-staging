For merchants · Track A

Know which agents are in your store.

AI shoppers are already browsing your catalog. Your logs show user-agent strings anyone can fake. We give you a straight answer to "who are these agents and what did they do?"

What you get

Verify agent traffic

Present an agent's credential and get a signed yes or no: who it is, who issued it, whether it is still valid. Free, no account needed to check.

See the traffic

A dashboard of identified agents: which ones visited, what they looked at, what they bought. The agents in your logs, finally legible.

Get alerted

Monitoring that flags unknown, revoked, or suspicious agents as they show up, so your team hears about it before it becomes a problem.

What it costs

Verification is free, forever. That is a design decision, not a promotion: the verifier only works if everyone uses it, so it will never cost anything to check a credential.

Visibility and monitoring are where we will eventually earn revenue. There is no pricing during the pilot.

Plainly stated: a verified agent is an identified agent, not a safe one. We attest to identity. We do not guarantee behavior, and we never will.

Start from your audit

If you have run a Checklane audit, you already know how legible your store is to AI shoppers. Identity is the natural next step: once you care about agent traffic, you need to know which agents it is. Audited merchants get first invites to the pilot.
