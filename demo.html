What this demo is

A sketch of the verification loop: present, check signature, check issuer, check expiry, check revocation, answer. The real verifier will do this against real issuers with a written threat model behind it. Until then, this page exists to show the shape of the answer: a signed yes or no, in under a second.

A passing check means the agent is who it claims to be. It never means the agent is safe.
